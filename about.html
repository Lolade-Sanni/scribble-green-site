Our Ecosystem

A Connected Network of Innovation

Scribblegreen operates within a collaborative ecosystem of purpose-driven organizations. Together with our sister companies, we combine research, education, media, branding, and digital communication to create meaningful solutions for individuals, institutions, and businesses—transforming ideas into lasting impact.

African Student Gazette

African Student Gazette is our digital media platform dedicated to informing, inspiring, and empowering students across Africa. Through articles, interviews, career insights, and campus stories, it delivers relevant content that helps students make informed decisions and discover new academic and professional opportunities.

Benign

Benign is our creative branding and marketing partner, helping organizations build memorable brands and stronger audience connections. Through brand strategy, visual storytelling, digital marketing, and strategic communications, Benign equips businesses with the creative tools to grow, engage, and stand out.
