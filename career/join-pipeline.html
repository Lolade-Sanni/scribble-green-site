Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior experience to volunteer? +

No — we welcome students and early-career contributors. We look for curiosity and reliability more than a finished résumé, and every volunteer is paired with a lead for guidance.

Is volunteering paid? +

Volunteer roles are unpaid but come with mentorship, references, and — where relevant — bylines or credited work. Freelance and open-application roles are compensated based on project scope.

Can a volunteer become a paid freelancer? +

Yes. Many of our freelancers started as volunteers. Strong volunteer contributors are often the first people we call when a paid brief opens up in their area.

How long does it take to hear back? +

Volunteer and freelance applications are typically reviewed within two weeks. Open applications are kept on file and may take longer, since we're matching against future rather than current needs.
